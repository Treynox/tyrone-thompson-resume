2) Technical Specialty (Grouped Skills)

3) Leadership Skills
- Leadership cards now reflect real management achievements from the resume:
  - Enterprise architecture leadership
  - Team mentorship and enablement
  - Program and delivery management

4) Case Studies (Named)

- Kept high contrast in all key text and interactive elements.

Design Constraints Preserved
